# Interview guides

Forty-five minutes each. Ask for the last real claim before asking for opinions, and watch the screen more than the speaker. No notes from these are in the repo, the guides are here to be used.

## Adjuster

1. Open the last claim you denied. Walk me through it from the first click.
2. Where did the clause come from? Show me the PDF and the search you typed.
3. Which clauses do you know by heart, and which do you look up every time?
4. When did you last change your mind on a claim, and what changed it?
5. If a draft were waiting when you opened a claim, what would make you stop reading it?

## Dealer service manager

1. Show me how a claim gets from the repair order into the system. Who types it?
2. Which field gets sent back most often?
3. Tell me about the last payout that was not what you expected. What did you do?
4. What do you keep outside the system because the system will not hold it?
5. Two people open the same claim in your shop. What happens?

## Finance controller

1. How do you build the reserve, and which claim fields feed it?
2. If one rule changed tomorrow, what would break in your month end?
3. Labor is rounded before the cap today, the manual says after. Which one is in your numbers?
4. What would you need to see before signing a change to a payout rule?
5. Who has asked for a rule change and been told no?

## Claims operations lead

1. What makes a week bad?
2. Which dealers call the most, and about what?
3. How many claims wait more than five days, and where do they wait?
4. What would you turn off if you could?
5. What would make you send a group back to the old system?
