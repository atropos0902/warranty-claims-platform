# Warranty policy manual

### 1.1 Standard coverage
Standard warranty covers defects in material and factory workmanship for the months shown in the terms table for the model. Coverage starts on the purchase date recorded by the selling dealer.

### 1.2 Extended coverage
Extended warranty applies only when the owner bought the plan before the standard warranty ended. A deductible is taken from labor and parts on every extended claim.

### 1.3 Reduced coverage
After the full coverage months, payment is reduced each month until the end month in the terms table. The reduction applies to labor and parts together.

### 1.4 Meter limit
A machine whose meter reading is above the limit for its model is out of coverage, whatever its age. A claim without a meter reading is held until the dealer supplies one.

### 1.5 Transfer to a new owner
The remaining standard warranty passes to a second owner if the transfer is registered within 30 days of sale. Extended warranty does not transfer.

### 2.1 Labor hours and caps
Labor hours are limited to the cap for the repair code and then rounded to the nearest half hour. Hours above the cap are not paid unless a field engineer approved them before the work started.

### 2.2 Diagnostic time
Diagnostic time is included in the labor cap and is not paid separately. An exception of one hour is paid when the fault needed a factory diagnostic session.

### 2.3 Overtime
Overtime and weekend labor are paid at the normal warranty rate. The difference is between the dealer and the customer.

### 2.4 Second technician
A second technician is paid only for repairs marked as two person lifts in the service manual. The helper is limited to half of the capped hours.

### 2.5 Repeat repair
A repeat of the same repair within 90 days of the first is not paid when the first repair was incomplete. The dealer carries the cost of its own workmanship.

### 3.1 Genuine parts
Only genuine parts ordered through the parts system are covered. Aftermarket or salvaged parts are not covered, and damage they cause is not covered either.

### 3.2 Parts handling
Dealers receive a handling allowance of ten percent on genuine parts, limited to 150 per claim. Handling is not paid on parts supplied free of charge.

### 3.3 Wear items
Filters, belts, wiper blades, brake linings and cutting edges are wear items and are not covered after the first 50 hours. A wear item that fails from a factory defect in its first 50 hours is covered.

### 3.4 Fluids and shop supplies
Oil, coolant and refrigerant lost because of a covered failure are covered in the amount needed to refill the system. Rags, cleaners and other shop supplies are not covered.

### 3.5 Return of failed parts
A failed part worth more than 500 must be kept for 60 days and returned when asked. A claim is denied when a requested part is not returned within 21 days.

### 3.6 Remanufactured units
A remanufactured unit may replace a failed assembly when the factory lists it for the model. The unit carries the rest of the original warranty.

### 4.1 Travel
Travel is paid for field repairs in steps of 25 miles, up to 200 miles for each claim. Travel to collect parts is not paid.

### 4.2 Hauling
Hauling a machine to the workshop is covered when it cannot be moved under its own power. Hauling for the convenience of the customer or the dealer is not covered.

### 4.3 Loaner machines
Loaner and rental machines are not covered under any warranty plan. Goodwill may be requested for a harvest emergency.

### 5.1 Hydraulic pump contamination
A hydraulic pump that failed because of contaminated oil is not covered when the filter service interval was missed. Oil sample results must be attached when contamination is suspected.

### 5.2 Hydraulic hoses
A hose that burst or leaked at a crimped fitting is covered as a factory defect. A hose cut, crushed or worn through by rubbing outside its clamps is not covered.

### 5.3 Hydraulic seals
Cylinder rod seals are covered for the first 12 months. Seals damaged by a bent or pitted rod are not covered.

### 5.4 Engine overheating
Engine damage from overheating is not covered when the radiator screen was blocked or the coolant was low. A failed thermostat or water pump that caused the overheating is covered together with the damage.

### 5.5 Fuel quality
Injector and fuel pump damage from water or dirt in the fuel is not covered. An injector that failed electrically is covered.

### 5.6 Cylinder head gasket
A cylinder head gasket failure is covered when the head surface is within the flatness limit. The measured flatness must be written in the repair notes.

### 5.7 Clutch pack
A clutch pack worn by towing or by riding the pedal is not covered. Clutch plates that separated or cracked at the hub are covered.

### 5.8 Transmission calibration
Transmission calibration is paid once after a covered clutch or valve repair. Calibration requested only for shift feel is not covered.

### 5.9 Wiring damage by rodents
Wiring damage caused by rodents or other animals is not covered. The photographs of the harness must be kept with the claim.

### 5.10 Connector corrosion
Corrosion inside a sealed connector is covered for the first 24 months. Corrosion caused by pressure washing is not covered.

### 5.11 Control modules
A control module is covered when the stored fault codes are attached to the claim. A module that was opened, jump started incorrectly or welded near without disconnection is not covered.

### 5.12 Software updates
A software update that corrects a published fault is paid at half an hour. Updates installed at the request of the customer are not paid.

### 5.13 Cab air conditioning
A compressor or condenser that failed internally is covered, including the refrigerant needed for recharge. Recharge alone is not covered because slow loss of refrigerant is maintenance.

### 5.14 Cab glass
Cab glass is not covered unless a stress crack starts at a mounting point. Impact damage is a matter for insurance.

### 5.15 Tires and tracks
Tires and rubber tracks are covered by their own makers and not by this warranty. The dealer sends the claim to the maker.

### 6.1 Unauthorized modification
Failures caused by a modification that the factory did not approve are not covered. Power increases to the engine end coverage of the engine and driveline.

### 6.2 Missed maintenance
A failure caused by missed maintenance is not covered. The dealer must attach the maintenance record when the engine, transmission or hydraulic system has failed.

### 6.3 Accident and misuse
Damage from accident, overload, flood or fire is not covered. Use outside the rated application of the machine is misuse.

### 6.4 Storage damage
Damage that happened in storage or transport before delivery is claimed against the carrier and not under warranty. Delivery inspection must record it.

### 7.1 Time to file
A claim must be filed within 30 days of the repair. Late claims are denied unless the dealer asked for an extension before the 30 days ended.

### 7.2 Goodwill
Goodwill may be paid for up to one year after coverage ends when the owner has used authorized service for all maintenance. Goodwill totals are paid in whole dollars.
